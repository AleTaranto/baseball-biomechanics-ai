# Glossary

## Athlete

The person whose movement is being analyzed.

## Biomechanics

The scientific study of movement, forces, and their relationship to performance and injury risk.

## Keypoint

A measurable anatomical or model-derived landmark used by motion analysis systems.

## Movement reconstruction

The process of turning raw motion data into a structured representation of the athlete's sequence of movement.

## Swing segmentation

The decomposition of a batting swing into distinct temporal phases.

## Recommendation

An actionable insight generated from metrics and interpretation rules, expressed in terms of observed movement quality rather than medical diagnosis.
